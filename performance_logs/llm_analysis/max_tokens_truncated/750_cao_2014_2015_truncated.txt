(Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance (Vereveningstoeslag): A supplement for employees working in business catering who were already employed by an employer in the sense of the CAO before July 1, 1997, representing the higher wage compared to the new wage scales that came into effect on July 1, 1997, for employees hired from July 1, 1997, and, for a maximum of 4 function year rounds, increased by an amount equal to the difference between the then-current function year and the subsequent function year in the scale applicable to this employee (based on the wage as indexed from the wage before the reduction for employees who joined an employer in the sense of the CAO only after June 30, 1997). The equalization allowance is a right arising from the CAO and is therefore not a supra-CAO right. (Article 1.14) Job classifications: The employer must use the Handboek Referentiefuncties Contractcatering (Handbook of Reference Functions Contract Catering) for job classification, as declared generally binding by decision in the Government Gazette of July 25, 2013, publication no. 17998, under UAW no. 11451. (Article 23) Function year scale: The adult employee receives at least the scale salary of the function year scale at 0 function years, belonging to the function group in which they are classified. (Article 27.2.a) Entry-placement rules: Employees new to the sector can be scaled into an entry-level salary scale for a maximum of six months. In salary groups I and II, this entry-level salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, it is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The minimum is the statutory minimum wage. (Article 27.2.a) If, due to previously acquired skills and experience, it would not be fair to grant the adult employee the scale salary at 0 function years upon entry, the employer may grant more function years. (Article 27.2.c) Progression within scales: Where there is a right to a function year, the scale salary is determined on January 1 of any year by granting a function year increase, as indicated in the scale salary, provided the employee was employed by the employer before October 1 of the preceding year. (Article 27.2.b) If the employee works for an employer who has introduced a performance appraisal system with the consent of the Works Council (or employee representation), a higher or lower wage increase than that corresponding to 1 function year may apply. (Article 27.2.b) An employee who has been incapacitated for work for 52 weeks or longer does not need to be granted function year increases during the period of incapacity. (Article 27.2.d) Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers are allowed to use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated application of a function year increase, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given its consent and the principles established by the CAO parties are incorporated. (Article 31.2) Consequences of appraisal: Insufficient = 0% function year increase; Moderate = 50%; Good = 100%; Excellent = 150%. For insufficient/moderate, an action plan must be drawn up. (Article 31.2) Exceptions: Employees who have reached the maximum number of function years and perform excellently receive a bonus of 50% of the periodic norm increase for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). A holiday worker can never perform work in scale 3 or higher and is not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO. (Article 28.1) Wage for temporary workers/payrollers: A temporary worker/payroller who is a skilled worker (vakkracht) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of employment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure that these temporary workers/payrollers receive wages and other reimbursements in accordance with this article. (Article 28.2) Uurloon (Hourly wage) calculation: The hourly wage for an employee is their monthly salary divided by 173.33, or for a 4-week period, divided by 160. (Article 1.13) Wage specification: The employer is obliged to calculate and pay the wage, including a wage specification, which must clearly state: a. Employee's name; b. Job title (from Jan 1, 2001); c. If possible, salary group and function year; d. Period of payment; e. Gross amount of wage and gratuity, including applicable allowances; f. CAO allowances, separately listed; g. Income tax deductions; h. Employee's share of social insurance premiums and Pension Fund Horeca & Catering; i. Employee's share of contributions to Fonds Bevordering Arbeidsverhoudingen and, if applicable, Fonds Bevordering Sociale Verhoudingen; j. Other amounts owed by the employee, precisely specified. (Article 33) Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4 month's salary, 1 month's salary, or 2 times the month's salary, respectively. The payment utilizes the maximum possible tax exemptions. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. (Article 23) Function year scale: The adult employee receives at least the scale salary of the function year scale at 0 function years, belonging to the function group in which they are classified. (Article 27.2.a) Entry-placement rules: Employees new to the sector can be scaled into an entry-level salary scale for a maximum of six months. In salary groups I and II, this entry-level salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, it is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The minimum is the statutory minimum wage. (Article 27.2.a) If, due to previously acquired skills and experience, it would not be fair to grant the adult employee the scale salary at 0 function years upon entry, the employer may grant more function years. (Article 27.2.c) Progression within scales: Where there is a right to a function year, the scale salary is determined on January 1 of any year by granting a function year increase, as indicated in the scale salary, provided the employee was employed by the employer before October 1 of the preceding year. (Article 27.2.b) If the employee works for an employer who has introduced a performance appraisal system with the consent of the Works Council (or employee representation), a higher or lower wage increase than that corresponding to 1 function year may apply. (Article 27.2.b) An employee who has been incapacitated for work for 52 weeks or longer does not need to be granted function year increases during the period of incapacity. (Article 27.2.d) Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers are allowed to use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated application of a function year increase, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given its consent and the principles established by the CAO parties are incorporated. (Article 31.1) Principles for performance appraisal: Annual 'result and development interview' and at least one 'progress interview'. Appraisal based on 3 to 5 objective criteria, plus individual criteria. Four-point scale: insufficient, moderate, good, excellent. (Article 31.2) Consequences of appraisal: Insufficient = 0% function year increase; Moderate = 50%; Good = 100%; Excellent = 150%. For insufficient/moderate, an action plan must be drawn up. (Article 31.2) Exceptions: Employees who have reached the maximum number of function years and perform excellently receive a bonus of 50% of the periodic norm increase for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic function year increase. (Article 31.2) Wage for holiday workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as holiday workers are determined by the Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968 (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). A holiday worker can never perform work in scale 3 or higher and is not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO. (Article 28.1) Wage for temporary workers/payrollers: A temporary worker/payroller who is a skilled worker (vakkracht) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of employment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure that these temporary workers/payrollers receive wages and other reimbursements in accordance with this article. (Article 28.2) Uurloon (Hourly wage) calculation: The hourly wage for an employee is their monthly salary divided by 173.33, or for a 4-week period, divided by 160. Job title (from Jan 1, 2001); c. If possible, salary group and function year; d. Period of payment; e. Gross amount of wage and gratuity, including applicable allowances; f. CAO allowances, separately listed; g. Income tax deductions; h. Employee's share of social insurance premiums and Pension Fund Horeca & Catering; i. Employee's share of contributions to Fonds Bevordering Arbeidsverhoudingen and, if applicable, Fonds Bevordering Sociale Verhoudingen; j. Other amounts owed by the employee, precisely specified. (Article 33) Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4 month's salary, 1 month's salary, or 2 times the month's salary, respectively. The payment utilizes the maximum possible tax exemptions. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. This personal allowance is part of the gross wage. As of July 1, 2011, this personal allowance will be phased out by 15% per year. The maximum duration of the phase-out scheme is 6 years and 8 months. For employees who were 55 years or older on July 1, 2006, the phase-out periods are doubled. The phase-out amount is paid out in a lump sum to the employee. (Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance (Vereveningstoeslag): A supplement for employees working in business catering who were already employed by an employer in the sense of the CAO before July 1, 1997, representing the higher wage compared to the new wage scales that came into effect on July 1, 1997, for employees hired from July 1, 1997, and, for a maximum of 4 function year rounds, increased by an amount equal to the difference between the then-current function year and the subsequent function year in the scale applicable to this employee (based on the wage as indexed from the wage before the reduction for employees who joined an employer in the sense of the CAO only after June 30, 1997). The equalization allowance is a right arising from the CAO and is therefore not a supra-CAO right. (Article 27.2.b) If the employee works for an employer who has introduced a performance appraisal system with the consent of the Works Council (or employee representation), a higher or lower wage increase than that corresponding to 1 function year may apply. (Article 27.2.b) An employee who has been incapacitated for work for 52 weeks or longer does not need to be granted function year increases during the period of incapacity. (Article 27.2.d) Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers are allowed to use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated application of a function year increase, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given its consent and the principles established by the CAO parties are incorporated. (Article 31.1) Principles for performance appraisal: Annual 'result and development interview' and at least one 'progress interview'. Appraisal based on 3 to 5 objective criteria, plus individual criteria. Four-point scale: insufficient, moderate, good, excellent. (Article 31.2) Consequences of appraisal: Insufficient = 0% function year increase; Moderate = 50%; Good = 100%; Excellent = 150%. For insufficient/moderate, an action plan must be drawn up. (Article 31.2) Exceptions: Employees who have reached the maximum number of function years and perform excellently receive a bonus of 50% of the periodic norm increase for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic function year increase. (Article 31.2) Wage for holiday workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as holiday workers are determined by the Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968 (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). A holiday worker can never perform work in scale 3 or higher and is not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO. (Article 28.1) Wage for temporary workers/payrollers: A temporary worker/payroller who is a skilled worker (vakkracht) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of employment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure that these temporary workers/payrollers receive wages and other reimbursements in accordance with this article. (Article 28.2) Uurloon (Hourly wage) calculation: The hourly wage for an employee is their monthly salary divided by 173.33, or for a 4-week period, divided by 160. (Article 1.13) Wage specification: The employer is obliged to calculate and pay the wage, including a wage specification, which must clearly state: a. Employee's name; b. Job title (from Jan 1, 2001); c. If possible, salary group and function year; d. Period of payment; e. Gross amount of wage and gratuity, including applicable allowances; f. CAO allowances, separately listed; g. Income tax deductions; h. Employee's share of social insurance premiums and Pension Fund Horeca & Catering; i. Employee's share of contributions to Fonds Bevordering Arbeidsverhoudingen and, if applicable, Fonds Bevordering Sociale Verhoudingen; j. Other amounts owed by the employee, precisely specified. (Article 33) Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4 month's salary, 1 month's salary, or 2 times the month's salary, respectively. The payment utilizes the maximum possible tax exemptions. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. This personal allowance is part of the gross wage. As of July 1, 2011, this personal allowance will be phased out by 15% per year. The maximum duration of the phase-out scheme is 6 years and 8 months. For employees who were 55 years or older on July 1, 2006, the phase-out periods are doubled. The phase-out amount is paid out in a lump sum to the employee. (Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance (Vereveningstoeslag): A supplement for employees working in business catering who were already employed by an employer in the sense of the CAO before July 1, 1997, representing the higher wage compared to the new wage scales that came into effect on July 1, 1997, for employees hired from July 1, 1997, and, for a maximum of 4 function year rounds, increased by an amount equal to the difference between the then-current function year and the subsequent function year in the scale applicable to this employee (based on the wage as indexed from the wage before the reduction for employees who joined an employer in the sense of the CAO only after June 30, 1997). The equalization allowance is a right arising from the CAO and is therefore not a supra-CAO right. (Article 1.14) Job classifications: The employer must use the Handboek Referentiefuncties Contractcatering (Handbook of Reference Functions Contract Catering) for job classification, as declared generally binding by decision in the Government Gazette of July 25, 2013, publication no. 17998, under UAW no. 11451. The employer establishes a company function by describing the main tasks and responsibilities assigned to the employee. The company function is classified into a function group based on comparison with reference functions. The employer must inform the employee of their function group and the reference function used, and include it in the written employment contract. (Article 23) Function year scale: The adult employee receives at least the scale salary of the function year scale at 0 function years, belonging to the function group in which they are classified. (Article 27.2.a) Entry-placement rules: Employees new to the sector can be scaled into an entry-level salary scale for a maximum of six months. If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic function year increase. (Article 31.2) Wage for holiday workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as holiday workers are determined by the Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968 (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). A holiday worker can never perform work in scale 3 or higher and is not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO. (Article 28.1) Wage for temporary workers/payrollers: A temporary worker/payroller who is a skilled worker (vakkracht) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of employment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure that these temporary workers/payrollers receive wages and other reimbursements in accordance with this article. (Article 28.2) Uurloon (Hourly wage) calculation: The hourly wage for an employee is their monthly salary divided by 173.33, or for a 4-week period, divided by 160. (Article 1.13) Wage specification: The employer is obliged to calculate and pay the wage, including a wage specification, which must clearly state: a. Employee's name; b. Job title (from Jan 1, 2001); c. If possible, salary group and function year; d. Period of payment; e. Gross amount of wage and gratuity, including applicable allowances; f. CAO allowances, separately listed; g. Income tax deductions; h. Employee's share of social insurance premiums and Pension Fund Horeca & Catering; i. Employee's share of contributions to Fonds Bevordering Arbeidsverhoudingen and, if applicable, Fonds Bevordering Sociale Verhoudingen; j. Other amounts owed by the employee, precisely specified. (Article 33) Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4 month's salary, 1 month's salary, or 2 times the month's salary, respectively. The payment utilizes the maximum possible tax exemptions. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. This personal allowance is part of the gross wage. As of July 1, 2011, this personal allowance will be phased out by 15% per year. The maximum duration of the phase-out scheme is 6 years and 8 months. For employees who were 55 years or older on July 1, 2006, the phase-out periods are doubled. The phase-out amount is paid out in a lump sum to the employee. (Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance (Vereveningstoeslag): A supplement for employees working in business catering who were already employed by an employer in the sense of the CAO before July 1, 1997, representing the higher wage compared to the new wage scales that came into effect on July 1, 1997, for employees hired from July 1, 1997, and, for a maximum of 4 function year rounds, increased by an amount equal to the difference between the then-current function year and the subsequent function year in the scale applicable to this employee (based on the wage as indexed from the wage before the reduction for employees who joined an employer in the sense of the CAO only after June 30, 1997). The equalization allowance is a right arising from the CAO and is therefore not a supra-CAO right. (Article 1.14) Job classifications: The employer must use the Handboek Referentiefuncties Contractcatering (Handbook of Reference Functions Contract Catering) for job classification, as declared generally binding by decision in the Government Gazette of July 25, 2013, publication no. 17998, under UAW no. 11451. The employer establishes a company function by describing the main tasks and responsibilities assigned to the employee. The employer must inform the employee of their function group and the reference function used, and include it in the written employment contract. (Article 23) Function year scale: The adult employee receives at least the scale salary of the function year scale at 0 function years, belonging to the function group in which they are classified. (Article 27.2.a) Entry-placement rules: Employees new to the sector can be scaled into an entry-level salary scale for a maximum of six months. In salary groups I and II, this entry-level salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, it is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The minimum is the statutory minimum wage. (Article 27.2.a) If, due to previously acquired skills and experience, it would not be fair to grant the adult employee the scale salary at 0 function years upon entry, the employer may grant more function years. (Article 27.2.c) Progression within scales: Where there is a right to a function year, the scale salary is determined on January 1 of any year by granting a function year increase, as indicated in the scale salary, provided the employee was employed by the employer before October 1 of the preceding year. (Article 27.2.b) If the employee works for an employer who has introduced a performance appraisal system with the consent of the Works Council (or employee representation), a higher or lower wage increase than that corresponding to 1 function year may apply. (Article 27.2.b) An employee who has been incapacitated for work for 52 weeks or longer does not need to be granted function year increases during the period of incapacity. (Article 28.2) Uurloon (Hourly wage) calculation: The hourly wage for an employee is their monthly salary divided by 173.33, or for a 4-week period, divided by 160. (Article 1.13) Wage specification: The employer is obliged to calculate and pay the wage, including a wage specification, which must clearly state: a. Employee's name; b. Job title (from Jan 1, 2001); c. If possible, salary group and function year; d. Period of payment; e. Gross amount of wage and gratuity, including applicable allowances; f. CAO allowances, separately listed; g. Income tax deductions; h. Employee's share of social insurance premiums and Pension Fund Horeca & Catering; i. Employee's share of contributions to Fonds Bevordering Arbeidsverhoudingen and, if applicable, Fonds Bevordering Sociale Verhoudingen; j. Other amounts owed by the employee, precisely specified. (Article 33) Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4 month's salary, 1 month's salary, or 2 times the month's salary, respectively. The payment utilizes the maximum possible tax exemptions. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. This personal allowance is part of the gross wage. As of July 1, 2011, this personal allowance will be phased out by 15% per year. The maximum duration of the phase-out scheme is 6 years and 8 months. For employees who were 55 years or older on July 1, 2006, the phase-out periods are doubled. The phase-out amount is paid out in a lump sum to the employee. (Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance (Vereveningstoeslag): A supplement for employees working in business catering who were already employed by an employer in the sense of the CAO before July 1, 1997, representing the higher wage compared to the new wage scales that came into effect on July 1, 1997, for employees hired from July 1, 1997, and, for a maximum of 4 function year rounds, increased by an amount equal to the difference between the then-current function year and the subsequent function year in the scale applicable to this employee (based on the wage as indexed from the wage before the reduction for employees who joined an employer in the sense of the CAO only after June 30, 1997). The equalization allowance is a right arising from the CAO and is therefore not a supra-CAO right. (Article 1.14) Job classifications: The employer must use the Handboek Referentiefuncties Contractcatering (Handbook of Reference Functions Contract Catering) for job classification, as declared generally binding by decision in the Government Gazette of July 25, 2013, publication no. 17998, under UAW no. 11451. The employer establishes a company function by describing the main tasks and responsibilities assigned to the employee. The company function is classified into a function group based on comparison with reference functions. The employer must inform the employee of their function group and the reference function used, and include it in the written employment contract. (Article 23) Function year scale: The adult employee receives at least the scale salary of the function year scale at 0 function years, belonging to the function group in which they are classified. (Article 27.2.a) Entry-placement rules: Employees new to the sector can be scaled into an entry-level salary scale for a maximum of six months. In salary groups I and II, this entry-level salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, it is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The minimum is the statutory minimum wage. (Article 27.2.a) If, due to previously acquired skills and experience, it would not be fair to grant the adult employee the scale salary at 0 function years upon entry, the employer may grant more function years. (Article 27.2.c) Progression within scales: Where there is a right to a function year, the scale salary is determined on January 1 of any year by granting a function year increase, as indicated in the scale salary, provided the employee was employed by the employer before October 1 of the preceding year. (Article 27.2.b) If the employee works for an employer who has introduced a performance appraisal system with the consent of the Works Council (or employee representation), a higher or lower wage increase than that corresponding to 1 function year may apply. (Article 27.2.b) An employee who has been incapacitated for work for 52 weeks or longer does not need to be granted function year increases during the period of incapacity. (Article 27.2.d) Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers are allowed to use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated application of a function year increase, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given its consent and the principles established by the CAO parties are incorporated. Appraisal based on 3 to 5 objective criteria, plus individual criteria. Four-point scale: insufficient, moderate, good, excellent. (Article 31.2) Consequences of appraisal: Insufficient = 0% function year increase; Moderate = 50%; Good = 100%; Excellent = 150%. For insufficient/moderate, an action plan must be drawn up. (Article 31.2) Exceptions: Employees who have reached the maximum number of function years and perform excellently receive a bonus of 50% of the periodic norm increase for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic function year increase. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. This personal allowance is part of the gross wage. As of July 1, 2011, this personal allowance will be phased out by 15% per year. The maximum duration of the phase-out scheme is 6 years and 8 months. For employees who were 55 years or older on July 1, 2006, the phase-out periods are doubled. The phase-out amount is paid out in a lump sum to the employee. (Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance (Vereveningstoeslag): A supplement for employees working in business catering who were already employed by an employer in the sense of the CAO before July 1, 1997, representing the higher wage compared to the new wage scales that came into effect on July 1, 1997, for employees hired from July 1, 1997, and, for a maximum of 4 function year rounds, increased by an amount equal to the difference between the then-current function year and the subsequent function year in the scale applicable to this employee (based on the wage as indexed from the wage before the reduction for employees who joined an employer in the sense of the CAO only after June 30, 1997). The equalization allowance is a right arising from the CAO and is therefore not a supra-CAO right. (Article 1.14) Job classifications: The employer must use the Handboek Referentiefuncties Contractcatering (Handbook of Reference Functions Contract Catering) for job classification, as declared generally binding by decision in the Government Gazette of July 25, 2013, publication no. 17998, under UAW no. 11451. The employer establishes a company function by describing the main tasks and responsibilities assigned to the employee. The company function is classified into a function group based on comparison with reference functions. The employer must inform the employee of their function group and the reference function used, and include it in the written employment contract. (Article 23) Function year scale: The adult employee receives at least the scale salary of the function year scale at 0 function years, belonging to the function group in which they are classified. (Article 27.2.a) Entry-placement rules: Employees new to the sector can be scaled into an entry-level salary scale for a maximum of six months. In salary groups I and II, this entry-level salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, it is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The minimum is the statutory minimum wage. (Article 27.2.a) If, due to previously acquired skills and experience, it would not be fair to grant the adult employee the scale salary at 0 function years upon entry, the employer may grant more function years. (Article 27.2.c) Progression within scales: Where there is a right to a function year, the scale salary is determined on January 1 of any year by granting a function year increase, as indicated in the scale salary, provided the employee was employed by the employer before October 1 of the preceding year. (Article 27.2.b) If the employee works for an employer who has introduced a performance appraisal system with the consent of the Works Council (or employee representation), a higher or lower wage increase than that corresponding to 1 function year may apply. (Article 27.2.b) An employee who has been incapacitated for work for 52 weeks or longer does not need to be granted function year increases during the period of incapacity. (Article 27.2.d) Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers are allowed to use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated application of a function year increase, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given its consent and the principles established by the CAO parties are incorporated. (Article 31.1) Principles for performance appraisal: Annual 'result and development interview' and at least one 'progress interview'. Appraisal based on 3 to 5 objective criteria, plus individual criteria. Four-point scale: insufficient, moderate, good, excellent. (Article 31.2) Consequences of appraisal: Insufficient = 0% function year increase; Moderate = 50%; Good = 100%; Excellent = 150%. For insufficient/moderate, an action plan must be drawn up. (Article 31.2) Exceptions: Employees who have reached the maximum number of function years and perform excellently receive a bonus of 50% of the periodic norm increase for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic function year increase. (Article 31.2) Wage for holiday workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as holiday workers are determined by the Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968 (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). A holiday worker can never perform work in scale 3 or higher and is not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO. (Article 28.1) Wage for temporary workers/payrollers: A temporary worker/payroller who is a skilled worker (vakkracht) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of employment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure that these temporary workers/payrollers receive wages and other reimbursements in accordance with this article. (Article 28.2) Uurloon (Hourly wage) calculation: The hourly wage for an employee is their monthly salary divided by 173.33, or for a 4-week period, divided by 160. (Article 1.13) Wage specification: The employer is obliged to calculate and pay the wage, including a wage specification, which must clearly state: a. Employee's name; b. Job title (from Jan 1, 2001); c. If possible, salary group and function year; d. Period of payment; e. Gross amount of wage and gratuity, including applicable allowances; f. CAO allowances, separately listed; g. Income tax deductions; h. Employee's share of social insurance premiums and Pension Fund Horeca & Catering; i. Employee's share of contributions to Fonds Bevordering Arbeidsverhoudingen and, if applicable, Fonds Bevordering Sociale Verhoudingen; j. Other amounts owed by the employee, precisely specified. (Article 33) Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4 month's salary, 1 month's salary, or 2 times the month's salary, respectively. The payment utilizes the maximum possible tax exemptions. (Article 48) Personal allowance for irregular hours (Business Catering): Employees who were working in a fixed schedule during irregular hours between 18:00 and 22:00 from Monday to Friday before April 1, 2008, and were employed before April 1, 2006, receive a personal allowance equal to the difference in the irregular hours allowance as it applies from July 1, 2006, compared to the irregular hours allowance as it applied immediately prior to July 1, 2006. The phase-out amount is paid out in a lump sum to the employee. (Article 4, Part A) Personal allowance for night shift (Inflightcatering): For employees of KLM Catering Services Schiphol B.V. to whom Article 5 of the Protocol dated February 16 and 20, 1996 ('Protocol') applied until July 1, 2012, the night shift allowance from Article 5 Protocol is replaced by the night shift allowance from Article 6 of Part C of this CAO. This night shift allowance is supplemented by a Personal Allowance equal to the amount of the allowance from Article 5 Protocol, applicable on June 30, 2012; this Personal Allowance is fixed as of July 1, 2012. The period for which the employee receives the personal allowance is equal to the number of years the employee worked under their protocol schedule until July 1, 2012; with a minimum of 5 years, where a part of a year is rounded up (e.g., 11 years and 3 months = 12 years). If the employment ends, including in case of retirement, the Personal Allowance also ends. If the employee is 55 years or older when the period mentioned above ends, Article 45 sub d of the CAO (2012-2013) applies analogously (i.e., not formally, but materially). (Article 6A, Part C) End-of-year bonus (Inflightcatering): In December, a performance-independent end-of-year bonus of 8.33% of the wage is paid out. (Article 10, Part C) Overplacement to higher group/scale: Upon definitive classification into a higher salary scale or function group, the adult employee's increase is as much as necessary to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement in the higher function group. (Article 29.a) Temporary function observation: An employee who temporarily performs the function of another employee classified in a higher wage group (other than due to vacation), if it is a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already included in their function, is entitled from the first day of observation to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the function they temporarily observe and their actual wage. (Article 29.b) A young employee who temporarily performs the function of another employee classified in a higher wage group (other than due to vacation), if it is a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already included in their function, is entitled from the first day of observation to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the function they temporarily observe and the CAO wage of their own function, taking age into account. (Article 29.c) Learning period for higher function: An employee who is given the opportunity to undergo a learning period with a view to placement in a higher function group (or higher salary scale) cannot claim a higher salary during that learning period. The learning period and its duration are communicated to the employee in writing. The learning period is a maximum of 3 months. (Article 29.d) Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to the original function level.
