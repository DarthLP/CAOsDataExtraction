For a 12.5-year service anniversary, 1/4 gross monthly salary is paid. Employees are entitled to a holiday allowance of 8% of the actual wage, excluding overtime payments and applicable allowances. This is typically paid in May over the preceding period. The applicable actual wages will be increased by: 3.0% as of September 1, 2023; 3.0% as of January 1, 2024; 2.0% as of July 1, 2024. An extra step is added to Wage Scale 1, 2 and 3, increasing them from 7 to 8 steps. These increases do not apply to auxiliary staff and employees with a work disability as mentioned in Article 6 Lid 1 and Lid 2. This classification does not apply to auxiliary staff (Article 6 Lid 1) or functions not listed in the function matrix. Adult apprentices are placed in the wage group corresponding to their function. Progression through entry steps is A to B to C, followed by periodic increment 0. Young apprentices start in entry step A. Instromers (new entrants) who do not follow a BBL training start in entry step B. Based on skills acquired elsewhere and/or experience gained, 1 or more periodic increments may be granted. The adult employee receives the CAO wage corresponding to their function at 0 periodic increments upon commencement of employment. If a performance-dependent remuneration system (Appendix XV) is applied, periodic progression depends on the appraisal system. If performance is very good, the employer may grant more periodic increments. If performance is insufficient, the employer may suspend the allocation of a periodic increment for a maximum of one year, with written notification of reasons. Each year on January 1, the adult employee receives an additional periodic increment, provided they have been employed for at least 6 months, until they reach the maximum of their wage scale. In deviation from the above, for young employees with child(ren) who are the sole income earner for their family: 17-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 19-year-olds; 18-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 20-year-olds; 19- and 20-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 21-year-olds. Adult apprentices are placed in the wage group corresponding to their function. Progression through entry steps is A to B to C, followed by periodic increment 0. Young apprentices start in entry step A. Instromers (new entrants) who do not follow a BBL training start in entry step B. Based on skills acquired elsewhere and/or experience gained, 1 or more periodic increments may be granted. The adult employee receives the CAO wage corresponding to their function at 0 periodic increments upon commencement of employment. If a performance-dependent remuneration system (Appendix XV) is applied, periodic progression depends on the appraisal system. If performance is very good, the employer may grant more periodic increments. If performance is insufficient, the employer may suspend the allocation of a periodic increment for a maximum of one year, with written notification of reasons. Each year on January 1, the adult employee receives an additional periodic increment, provided they have been employed for at least 6 months, until they reach the maximum of their wage scale. In deviation from the above, for young employees with child(ren) who are the sole income earner for their family: 17-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 19-year-olds; 18-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 20-year-olds; 19- and 20-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 21-year-olds. The CAO wages for young employees are determined based on the following percentages of the entry step or the lowest periodic increment of the function scale: 16 years and younger: 57.5%; 17 years: 62.5%; 18 years: 70%; 19 years: 80%; 20 years: 90%. Upon the death of an employee, the employer will pay 1 month's salary according to Article 7:674 BW plus 1 month's extra-statutory salary, as long as this is fiscally possible. For a 40-year service anniversary, 1 net monthly salary is paid. For a 25-year service anniversary, 1 net monthly salary is paid. For a 12.5-year service anniversary, 1/4 gross monthly salary is paid. Employees are entitled to a holiday allowance of 8% of the actual wage, excluding overtime payments and applicable allowances. This is typically paid in May over the preceding period. The applicable actual wages will be increased by: 3.0% as of September 1, 2023; 3.0% as of January 1, 2024; 2.0% as of July 1, 2024. An extra step is added to Wage Scale 1, 2 and 3, increasing them from 7 to 8 steps. These increases do not apply to auxiliary staff and employees with a work disability as mentioned in Article 6 Lid 1 and Lid 2. Employee functions are classified into function groups based on the ORBA job evaluation system, as listed in the 'Functiewaardering groene sector' handbook and the function grid in Appendix XII. This classification does not apply to auxiliary staff (Article 6 Lid 1) or functions not listed in the function matrix. If performance is insufficient, the employer may suspend the allocation of a periodic increment for a maximum of one year, with written notification of reasons. Each year on January 1, the adult employee receives an additional periodic increment, provided they have been employed for at least 6 months, until they reach the maximum of their wage scale. In deviation from the above, for young employees with child(ren) who are the sole income earner for their family: 17-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 19-year-olds; 18-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 20-year-olds; 19- and 20-year-olds are entitled to the CAO wage of 21-year-olds. The CAO wages for young employees are determined based on the following percentages of the entry step or the lowest periodic increment of the function scale: 16 years and younger: 57.5%; 17 years: 62.5%; 18 years: 70%; 19 years: 80%; 20 years: 90%. Upon the death of an employee, the employer will pay 1 month's salary according to Article 7:674 BW plus 1 month's extra-statutory salary, as long as this is fiscally possible. For a 40-year service anniversary, 1 net monthly salary is paid. For a 25-year service anniversary, 1 net monthly salary is paid. For a 12.5-year service anniversary, 1/4 gross monthly salary is paid. Employees are entitled to a holiday allowance of 8% of the actual wage, excluding overtime payments and applicable allowances.
