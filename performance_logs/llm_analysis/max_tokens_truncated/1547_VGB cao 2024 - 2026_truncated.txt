A phase-out of the personal allowance due to business circumstances can only occur after consultation with the employee representation body or, in its absence, after consultation with a trade union that is a party to this CAO. Business circumstances do not include a harmonization of employment conditions as a result of a company takeover. Youth scales are for ages 16-20 and are not included as per instructions. The full-time weekly hours baseline is not explicitly stated, but derived as 40 hours/week in the hourly salary calculation. Holiday allowance is not explicitly stated as included in the amounts. The CAO salaries and salary scales will be changed as follows during the term of the CAO: As of January 1, 2025, salaries and salary scales are increased by 3%. As of January 1, 2026, salaries and salary scales are increased by 3%. As of July 1, 2026, salaries and salary scales are increased by 1%. The year-end bonus is 1% of the total monthly or period income earned in the calendar year. Night allowance is 26% of the gross hourly wage for hours worked between 23:00 and 06:00. Intern allowance is at least €350 gross per month for full-time internships. If the statutory minimum wage exceeds the CAO salaries, the employer is obliged to pay the statutory minimum wage. The period salary is 12/13 of the monthly salary. The hourly salary is 0.577% of the monthly salary (monthly salary multiplied by 3 divided by 13 weeks divided by 40). Monthly income is monthly salary plus fixed allowances, excluding expense reimbursements. Annual income is 12x monthly income or 13x period income plus holiday allowance, year-end bonus, and other fixed payments/allowances. The individual differentiated increase for employees not yet at the end salary is 1% for normal functioning, 2% for good functioning, and 0% for substandard functioning, effective January 1, 2025, and January 1, 2026. If no assessment system is agreed upon, the increase is 1% of the scale salary for employees not yet at the end of the scale, effective January 1, 2025, and January 1, 2026. A phase-out of the personal allowance due to business circumstances can only occur after consultation with the employee representation body or, in its absence, after consultation with a trade union that is a party to this CAO. Business circumstances do not include a harmonization of employment conditions as a result of a company takeover. Youth scales are for ages 16-20 and are not included as per instructions. The full-time weekly hours baseline is not explicitly stated, but derived as 40 hours/week in the hourly salary calculation. Holiday allowance is not explicitly stated as included in the amounts. The CAO salaries and salary scales will be changed as follows during the term of the CAO: As of January 1, 2025, salaries and salary scales are increased by 3%. As of January 1, 2026, salaries and salary scales are increased by 3%. As of July 1, 2026, salaries and salary scales are increased by 1%. The year-end bonus is 1% of the total monthly or period income earned in the calendar year. Night allowance is 26% of the gross hourly wage for hours worked between 23:00 and 06:00. Intern allowance is at least €350 gross per month for full-time internships. If the statutory minimum wage exceeds the CAO salaries, the employer is obliged to pay the statutory minimum wage. The period salary is 12/13 of the monthly salary. The hourly salary is 0.577% of the monthly salary (monthly salary multiplied by 3 divided by 13 weeks divided by 40). Monthly income is monthly salary plus fixed allowances, excluding expense reimbursements. Annual income is 12x monthly income or 13x period income plus holiday allowance, year-end bonus, and other fixed payments/allowances. The individual differentiated increase for employees not yet at the end salary is 1% for normal functioning, 2% for good functioning, and 0% for substandard functioning, effective January 1, 2025, and January 1, 2026. If no assessment system is agreed upon, the increase is 1% of the scale salary for employees not yet at the end of the scale, effective January 1, 2025, and January 1, 2026. If employment starts within 4 months before the next increase date, the increase may be postponed.
