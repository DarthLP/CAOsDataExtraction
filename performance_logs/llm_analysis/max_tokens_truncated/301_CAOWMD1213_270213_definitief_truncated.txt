Personal allowances at the maximum of a scale: The salary scales include 'uitloopperiodieken' (outflow periodiek numbers) U1 and U2, which allow for exceeding the maximum of the salary scale by up to two periodiek numbers under specific conditions related to appraisal (Article 4.4.4). General wage increases: 1.0% salary increase effective December 1, 2012. 1.5% salary increase effective February 1, 2013. Holiday allowance: 8% of the gross salary actually earned and/or the wage continuation/supplement as referred to in Article 11.2 during the holiday allowance year (June 1 to May 31). Minimum amounts: €148.64 per month from May 1, 2010; €150.13 per month from December 1, 2012; €152.38 per month from February 1, 2013. Paid proportionally for partial year employment. Payment occurs no later than May. Year-end bonus: 8.3% of the gross salary actually earned in the calendar year, plus the holiday allowance accrued in that year. Minimum amounts: €1,095.16 from December 2012; €1,110.22 from December 2013 for full-time employment. Paid proportionally for partial employment. Special rules apply for employees aged 55 or older on January 1, 2009, or who turned 55 in 2010, who opted to use the year-end bonus increase for extra senior leave hours (resulting in 3.5% or 5.5% year-end bonus, respectively). Paid in December, or earlier if employment ends. Jubilee bonus: Employees with 25, 40, or 50 years of service (with one or more employers under this CAO or related CAOs) are entitled to a jubilee gratuity: half a month's salary for 25 years, a full month's salary for 40 years, and a full month's salary for 50 years. The 'month's salary' for this purpose includes gross monthly salary, one month's holiday allowance, and the average monthly irregular service allowance (Article 5.2.1) over the 3 months preceding the jubilee. One-off payment: Employees in service on July 1, 2013, will receive a one-off gross payment of €300.00 in July 2013, proportional to their employment. Allowance for acting in a higher-paid position: An employee temporarily (fully or almost fully) acting in a higher-paid position (other than for vacation leave) receives an allowance. This allowance is granted retroactively to the start of the acting period once it has lasted 30 days. The allowance is the difference between their current salary and the salary they would receive if classified in the higher position. If multiple employees share the acting role, the allowance is proportional to the percentage of the role assigned. Labor market allowance: The employer may grant a labor market allowance to (groups of) employees, up to a maximum of 10% of the salary. This allowance is granted for one year and can be extended annually. A scheme for granting this allowance requires the consent of the Works Council (OR). Hourly wage calculation: The gross hourly wage is obtained by dividing the gross monthly amount corresponding to a periodiek number in the salary tables (Bijlage 2) by 156. The salary amounts apply to a full-time employment of 36 hours per week. Periodiek numbers 0 to 13 correspond to the initial salary placement. Outflow periodiek numbers U1 and U2 are subject to Article 4.4, paragraph 4. Guarantee numbers a to e are subject to Article A of Bijlage 9.3. Job classifications and salary scales: The functiematrix (Bijlage 1) links job titles to salary scales. The Function Book (Bijlage 12) provides detailed descriptions for these matrix functions. If a function does not fully match a description, the salary is determined by comparing it to functions in the Function Book or by using the Methodical Level Assignment (MNT) method for Welfare. Annual salary increase: The employee's salary is increased annually by one periodieknummer until the maximum of the applicable salary scale for their function is reached. This periodic increase is granted on January 1 or on the first day of the calendar month in which the employee started employment, reached 18 years of age (when youth scales applied), or was promoted to a higher function. The specific date is stated in the individual employment contract; if not, January 1 applies. Progression within scales: The employer can withhold or grant extra periodiek numbers only if an appraisal scheme is in place. This must be communicated to the employee in writing and with reasons in advance. Granting extra periodiek numbers can exceed the maximum of the salary scale by a maximum of two periodiek numbers (U1 and U2). Entry-placement rules: Upon commencement of employment, a salary is assigned within the salary scale corresponding to the function. If the employee is appointed to a function requiring training and is still in training, Articles 4.2.3a and 4.2.3b apply.
