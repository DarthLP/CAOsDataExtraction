## v0 discrepancies the auditor flagged (input → these are the defects)

- **high** `other`: For jobgroups '0'-'10' the structured rows contain the text's Trede ROW read across the scale columns (e.g. jobgroup='0' = Trede=0 across; jobgroup='10' = Trede=10 across, which has only 10 populated scales). This is the wrong axis encoding — jobgroup should hold the scale, step the Trede.
- **high** `other`: Exact match for Trede=10 read across, confirming the axis-swap pattern for jobgroups 0-10.
- **high** `row_duplication_or_fabrication`: Jobgroup='11' switches axis convention mid-record — first 10 steps follow scale=11 column down, then steps 10-11 are fabricated by lifting two cells from the Trede=11 ROW (5439 and 8343). Same hybrid pattern appears in jobgroups 12 (step=10=4670, step=11=8607 are from Trede=12 row), 13 (step=10=8881
- **high** `wrong_axis_encoding`: Jobgroups 11-18 use the correct orientation (jobgroup=scale, step=trede), while jobgroups 0-10 use the swapped orientation (jobgroup=trede, step=scale). This is internally inconsistent and means downstream consumers cannot tell what any given jobgroup label refers to.
- **low** `missing_scale`: Because the structured uses two different axis conventions, cells in the top-right quadrant of the matrix (high-Trede × low-Scale or vice versa) are partially captured, partially missing. 4522 (Trede=11 scale=9) is one example.
