\section{Stage 2: Non-Salary Correction and Verification}
\label{sec:correction}

\subsection{Stage-0 Ingest and Canonical Artifact Architecture}

Stage~2 of the pipeline systematically audits and corrects upstream non-salary extractions across 34 structured correction layers ($L_1 \rightarrow L_{34}$, corresponding to generations $G_0 \rightarrow G_{33}$ documented in \nolinkurl{verification_pipeline/docs/DATA_LINEAGE.md}). The canonical dataset is:
\begin{center}
\nolinkurl{verification_pipeline/qa/corrected_dataset.csv}
\end{center}
This canonical table contains exactly \textbf{2,739 rows, 318 columns, and 242 unique collective agreements} (\texttt{cao\_number}), formatted as semicolon-delimited (\texttt{;}) UTF-8 text. It reflects Generation $G_{33}$---the final cumulative state following thirty-four sequential verification layers over the raw upstream extract ($G_0$). All Stage~3 derived indices and downstream panel regressions consume this file directly.

The correction architecture strictly enforces data immutability through a four-stage state-transition protocol:
\begin{center}
\textbf{apply $\longrightarrow$ copy $\longrightarrow$ verify $\longrightarrow$ promote}
\end{center}
No correction is ever applied directly in place. All candidate modifications are captured in immutable changelogs, validated against programmatic integrity gates, and promoted only after passing comprehensive regression suites.

\subsection{The 34-Layer Correction Lineage (Generations G0--G33)}

\paragraph{What every layer tests.} The unit under test is a single \emph{cell}: the pair (record, field), where a record is one edition of one agreement and a field is one of the 318 schema columns. Across all 34 layers the adjudication question is invariant:

\begin{center}
\emph{Does this edition's own source text state this value \textbf{explicitly}, under \textbf{this field's} schema definition?}
\end{center}

Each cell receives one of three verdicts: \texttt{KEEP} (supported), \texttt{FIX} (unsupported---corrected to the stated value, or blanked / set \texttt{False}), or \texttt{CANT\_TELL} (undecidable at the evidence tier available, escalated to the next tier).\footnote{Two rules bound the verdict space. First, the grounding doctrine: if the document is silent, or says ``statutory'' without naming a figure, the cell is left empty---values are never derived, annualized, summed, or inferred from legislation. Second, the evidence tier: verification reads keyword snippets, then the full topic extract, then the parsed full-text markdown, then re-OCR'd PDF text, and a higher tier always outranks a lower one. Snippet-only reading alone produced a $\approx 97\%$ false-absence rate (\S\ref{subsec:methodology-lessons}), so extract-silence is never treated as document-silence.} What varies between layers is therefore not the question but two operational choices: \textbf{which cells are nominated as suspect} (the detector), and \textbf{which evidence tier} the reader is permitted to consult.

\paragraph{The detectors.} Suspicion is generated by five mechanisms:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{3.1cm}Xp{2.3cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Detector} & \textbf{Suspicion logic} & \textbf{Layers} \\
\midrule
Deterministic rules & Cell-local violations: impossible percentages ($>100$), unit-family mismatch, year-like value in a duration field, article number captured as a value, decimal-point strip, placeholder evidence & $L_1$, $L_3$--$L_7$, $L_{29}$ \\
Cross-edition flip & A boolean or numeric changes between editions of one agreement; at most one side can be correct & $L_8$ \\
Same-term discrepancy & Two editions of the \emph{same} term (a republication) disagree; a difference with no legal cause is an extraction error & $L_9$, $L_{10}$, $L_{20}$ \\
Index-jump anomaly & $|\Delta z| \ge 0.5$ between editions, partitioned into tier~A (same-term), tier~B ($V/\Lambda$ sandwich: a value spikes and reverts, so the middle edition is the suspect), and tier~C (one-way jumps, reverting or persisting) & $L_{14}$--$L_{28}$ \\
Family consistency & Records sharing (\texttt{cao\_number}, \texttt{ingangsdatum}) should carry identical values & $L_{15}$--$L_{19}$, $L_{32}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Two layers fall outside this scheme: $L_{23}$ re-extracts entire documents whose upstream parse was fabrication-riddled, and $L_{30} \rightarrow L_{31}$ re-audits by \emph{error direction} after a stratified random sample revealed that errors were one-sided (\S\ref{subsec:methodology-lessons}).

\paragraph{What counts as an error.} The corrections resolve seven recurring defect classes: \textbf{phantom True} (a boolean asserting a provision the document never grants---the single largest class); \textbf{false absence} (a provision present in the document but absent from the extract); \textbf{field-boundary misrouting} (a genuine clause credited to the wrong field);\footnote{Confirmed systemic mis-routings include \textit{ploegentoeslag} (shift allowance) booked as a job allowance, \textit{calamiteitenverlof} (emergency leave) as care leave, absence registration as workload monitoring, and clauses restricted to niche groups (youth, apprentices, 55+, AOW-age) credited to the typical worker.} \textbf{unit and denominator errors} (most consequentially, percent-of-premium stored as percent-of-salary, the 1,071-cell $L_{13}$ correction); \textbf{invented numbers} (derived or statutory-inferred figures appearing nowhere verbatim); \textbf{template shadowing} (an unfilled model-contract appendix read as an agreed term); and \textbf{corrupt parse} (whole extractions hallucinated from defective OCR).

Two things are explicitly \emph{not} tested. The layers do not adjudicate the legal merit of an agreement's provisions, and they never consult statutory baselines to \emph{source} a value: a value matching its era's statutory constant is a suspicion flag only (the standing fingerprint queue described in \S\ref{subsec:methodology-lessons}), never evidence for filling a cell.\footnote{This also delimits the residual risk. Four of the five detectors require cross-record \emph{disagreement} in order to fire, so an error replicated identically across every edition of an agreement is invisible to all of them---the structural motivation for the standing cross-sectional fingerprint queue described in \S\ref{subsec:methodology-lessons}.}

The 34 layers were executed in four historical waves, summarised below by layer, generation range, and cells changed.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Wave 1 --- per-topic QA and schema audits ($L_1$--$L_7$, $G_0 \rightarrow G_6$, $\sim$9,200 cells).} Deterministic-rule and human review over 95 core agreements: 514 cells ($L_1$); 781 net cells from twice-verified holistic and definition-anchored review ($L_2$); 3,318 quote-backed per-file fixes ($L_3$); 2,332 verified absence removals ($L_4$---1,813 boolean demotions and 519 enum normalizations, with numerics carved out after a 100-cell pilot showed an 83\% false-removal rate on numbers); and 2,105 cells adjudicated under six written conventions---base figure, surcharge increment, first tier, representation equality, strict enums, quote evidence ($L_5$; 12 subagents at 57/60 spot-check agreement, plus a human ruling sheet retiring 22 patterns). $L_6$--$L_7$ cleared 151 flagged patterns and a 40-cell residue queue.
  \item \textbf{Wave 2 --- cross-edition and same-term consistency ($L_8$--$L_{13}$, $G_7 \rightarrow G_{12}$, $\sim$4,800 cells).} 3,559 flip inconsistencies (3,549 boolean, 10 numeric) resolved by multi-reader majority vote against source text ($L_8$); 61 same-term numeric fixes surviving from 668 candidates after a second reader rejected half ($L_9$); 111 unifications, tie-breaks and full-passage recoveries, including 7 unit-blind clobbers reverted and all 30 residual ties broken ($L_{10}$); 36 date-collision discrepancies ($L_{11}$--$L_{12}$); and the 1,071-cell pension denominator blanking ($L_{13}$).
  \item \textbf{Wave 3 --- jump campaigns, family sweeps, re-extraction, ripple closure ($L_{14}$--$L_{28}$, $G_{13} \rightarrow G_{27}$, $\sim$5,900 cells).} Family, secondary-mover and topic sweeps ($L_{14}$--$L_{19}$; 77 family-consistency cells including the CAO 1022 childcare phantoms, 400 cells across 162 families, 14 ambiguous cells settled on text identity); the tier~A same-term campaign with word-level quote provenance (902 cells over 645 jump events, $L_{20}$); 106 \texttt{CANT\_TELL} cells escalated to full text and re-OCR ($L_{21}$); 22 human rulings on residual pay tiers and probation caps ($L_{22}$); full re-extraction of the two corrupt-parse agreements, CAO 3313004 and CAO 310005 (252 cells, $L_{23}$); the tier~B sandwich (461 cells), tier~C reverting (1,410) and tier~C persistent (1,465) campaigns ($L_{24}$--$L_{26}$); and six ripple waves driving uncovered sibling sides to a fixed point ($662 \rightarrow 168 \rightarrow 44 \rightarrow 22 \rightarrow 11 \rightarrow 7 \rightarrow 0$; 713 cells, $L_{27}$--$L_{28}$).
  \item \textbf{Wave 4 --- closure and restorative re-verification ($L_{29}$--$L_{34}$, $G_{28} \rightarrow G_{33}$, $\sim$1,000 cells).} 274 post-campaign cells, including full-text closure on 435 \texttt{CANT\_TELL} cells and a deterministic part-time screen over all 2,739 records ($L_{29}$); the 39-cell stratified audit with Opus arbitration of 10 disputes ($L_{30}$), whose directional finding forced re-verification of all 1,628 standing restorations and blank-to-value fills under strict field-boundary discipline, changing 516 cells---220 de-restorations, 155 numeric/blank fixes, 141 companion units ($L_{31}$); 132 remaining \texttt{CANT\_TELL} cells closed by family consistency and markdown-tier full-text gates ($L_{32}$--$L_{33}$); and 37 follow-up flags, among them the CAO 759 quarterly cap, CAO 3768 AOW restatements, CAO 1214 December bonus unit relabeling and CAO 1029 maritime shift routing ($L_{34}$)---yielding the canonical $G_{33}$ dataset.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Active Automated Regression Test Suite}
\label{subsec:regression-suite}

To guarantee ongoing pipeline integrity and prevent regressions across schema revisions, the repository maintains an active automated regression test suite that re-checks the correction layers code's logic:
\begin{center}
\texttt{cd verification\_pipeline \&\& python3 -m pytest qa -q}
\end{center}
The suite executes \textbf{152 automated regression tests} across 13 test modules, running cleanly in under 5 seconds with zero failures. The test battery is partitioned into two specialized directories:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Full Audit Integrity Tests} (\nolinkurl{verification_pipeline/qa/full_audit/tests/}, 59 tests): Validates multi-edition agreement consistency (\texttt{test\_agreement\_consistency.py}, 10 tests), cross-version difference tracking (\texttt{test\_cross\_version.py}, 11 tests), enum string canonicalization against schema rules (\texttt{test\_enum\_format.py}, 15 tests), numeric distribution bounds and plausibility clamps (\texttt{test\_outliers\_numeric.py}, 7 tests), unit family semantic compatibility (\texttt{test\_unit\_semantics.py}, 9 tests), and value-unit contamination separation (\texttt{test\_value\_unit\_contamination.py}, 7 tests).
  \item \textbf{Shared Component and Invariant Tests} (\nolinkurl{verification_pipeline/qa/shared/tests/}, 93 tests): Validates winner-selection logic in rule arbitration (\texttt{test\_aggregator.py}, 14 tests), 18 data invariant rules covering whitespace, placeholder leakage, and unparseable units (\texttt{test\_audit.py}, 29 tests), CSV delimiter splitting recovery and quote repair (\texttt{test\_csv\_recovery.py}, 7 tests), statutory floors and caps across historical legislation eras (\texttt{test\_era\_baselines.py}, 21 tests), section slicing and topic mapping in source loaders (\texttt{test\_source\_text\_loader.py}, 13 tests), Dutch spelling variants and decimal comma handling (\texttt{test\_value\_variants.py}, 4 tests), and worksheet context token budgets (\texttt{test\_worksheet\_builder.py}, 5 tests).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Worksheet Modes and Context Budgets}

Verification tasks are partitioned into subagent worksheets managed by \texttt{worksheet\_builder.py}. Each item is presented in one of three modes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Extract}: Field is currently empty; subagent searches source text for explicit numbers.
  \item \textbf{Blind}: A deterministic rule flagged the cell, but the proposed value is hidden; the subagent inspects source text independently.
  \item \textbf{Informed}: The subagent is presented with the existing value and the proposed change as a hypothesis to test skeptically.
\end{itemize}

Worksheet chunking enforces hard token caps: $\le 3{,}500$ static system prompt tokens, 2,000--4,000 tokens per item context, and $\le 50{,}000$ total chunk context (15--20 items per chunk).

\subsection{Multi-Agent Verification Protocol}

To eliminate single-agent hallucination and bias, corrections follow a three-tier arbitration chain:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Proposer}: Generates candidate corrections backed by verbatim quotes from the English source text.
  \item \textbf{Skeptical Second Reader}: An independent subagent tasked specifically with attempting to falsify the proposed correction.
  \item \textbf{Opus Arbiter}: High-capacity model (\texttt{claude-opus}) acting as final judge on disputed cases or complex legal clauses (especially in pensions and termination).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Methodological Lessons}
\label{subsec:methodology-lessons}

Three failure modes recurred often enough to change the protocol:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Extract-silence is not document-silence.} Judging a cell from extracted snippets alone produced a $\approx 97\%$ false-absence rate: the reader saw absence in the snippet, not in the document. No verdict may rest below the full-document tier.
  \item \textbf{Definition-less drift.} Without descriptions in the prompt, $\approx 22\%$ of proposed corrections erred in the same way---payment percentages routed into duration fields, statutory references read as agreed terms.
  \item \textbf{Deletion bias.} An unconstrained reader treats a non-standard unit as an error. Preservation-aware rules kept 269 of 308 unit-semantics cells (87\% \texttt{KEEP}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Residual State of the Canonical Dataset}
Three figures describe where the corpus stands at $G_{33}$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Log fidelity.} The $L_{30}$ mechanical audit replayed every logged change against the pre-$L_{20}$ backup: zero replay mismatches, zero unlogged changes, total churn 0.9\%.
  \item \textbf{Noise level.} The boolean flip rate --- how often a single yes/no field disagrees between two printings of the same agreement (\nolinkurl{boolean_flip_rate.py}) --- stands at a mean of 3.3\%, down from 9.1\% before the jump campaigns; the residue is individually verified draft-versus-final difference.
  \item \textbf{Open queues.} 28 cells the full text cannot settle remain in \nolinkurl{indices/review/open_cant_tells_for_hanna.csv} as a human decision queue, and roughly 1,150 statutory-fingerprint suspects have never been agent-checked --- the standing worklist for the blind spot above, where an error replicated identically across every edition triggers no disagreement detector.
\end{itemize}
