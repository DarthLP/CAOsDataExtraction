\subsection{Project Scope and Objective}

In the Netherlands, collective labour agreements (\emph{Collectieve Arbeidsovereenkomsten}, CAOs) govern employment terms, salary scales, working hours, leave entitlements, pensions, and workplace conditions for the vast majority of employees. These legal instruments are published as PDF documents by the Ministry of Social Affairs and Employment (SZW) on the portal \texttt{uitvoeringarbeidsvoorwaardenwetgeving.nl}. 

The objective of this project is to construct an auditable, deterministic, and empirically rigorous pipeline that:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em]
  \item Scrapes and parses more than 1,500 historical and active CAO PDF documents;
  \item Extracts structured clauses across 13 distinct thematic domains (wages, pensions, leave, working hours, contract types, training, severance, etc.);
  \item Parses multi-tier salary grids deterministically without hallucination risk;
  \item Audits and corrects upstream extraction errors through an audited 34-layer verification protocol;
  \item Derives standardised generosity indices (pooled-z and percentile tracks) and nominal wage ladders anchored to statutory minimum wage schedules;
  \item Produces a balanced monthly in-force panel workbook for empirical econometric research.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Unified Pipeline Layout}

The project integrates five sequential stages under a unified layout, resolving legacy separations between extraction, salary parsing, and dataset verification:

\begin{figure}[h!]
\centering
\begin{verbatim}
Stage 1: Extraction (pipelines/)
  |-- p1_webscraping.py        -> Download PDFs from official SZW portal
  |-- p2_extract.py            -> Multi-method text extraction (PyPDF2 + OCR)
  |-- p3_llmExtraction.py      -> First-pass Gemini extraction into 13 topics
  |-- p4_analysis.py           -> Schema-enforced Pydantic structured output
  \-- p5_excel_creation.py     -> Merged tabular outputs (extracted_data_non_salary.csv)

Stage 1b: Deterministic Salary Parsing (salary_parser/)
  \-- deliver.py               -> Parses p3 wage tables deterministically
                                  (100% amount provenance, confidence tiers A-D)

Stage 2: Non-Salary Correction & Verification (verification_pipeline/qa/)
  \-- apply_corrections.py     -> 34 audited layers (G0->G33), ~12,000 corrections,
                                  grounded against English-translated source text

Stage 3: Derived Indices & Panel Generation (verification_pipeline/indices/)
  \-- rebuild.sh               -> Pooled-z generosity scores, statutory floor,
                                  wage index, monthly in-force panel, 26-tab workbook

Stage 4: Documentation & Verification Reports (Reports/Pipeline/)
  \-- report/CAOPipeline.tex   -> Modular replication guide and technical documentation
\end{verbatim}
\caption{End-to-end architecture of the unified CAO extraction and verification pipeline.}
\label{fig:pipeline-arch}
\end{figure}
