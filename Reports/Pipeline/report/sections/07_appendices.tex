\section{LLM Model Tiering and Calibration}
\label{app:models}

Model selection follows a validated capability-cost hierarchy:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Gemini 2.5 Flash (\texttt{gemini-2.5-flash})}: Ingest extraction (p3) and initial schema analysis (p4). High context capacity (1M tokens) enables ingesting full multi-page CAO chapters.
  \item \textbf{Claude Haiku}: Default model for routine subagent review worksheets and boolean presence checks. Fast, low cost.
  \item \textbf{Claude Sonnet}: Calibrated for complex multi-condition verification. In testing, Haiku was equally safe but 54\% over-cautious when judging snippet absences; Sonnet was selected for all snippet-to-full-text arbitration.
  \item \textbf{Claude Opus}: Standing arbiter for pension clause interpretations, statutory chain-rule (\emph{ketenregeling}) deviations, and termination legal disputes.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked Example: End-to-End CAO Tracking}
\label{app:worked-example}

To illustrate the transformations applied by the pipeline, consider CAO 1022 (Secondary Vocational Education, \emph{MBO}):
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Raw Document}: The base collective agreement is \nolinkurl{inputs/pdfs/input_pdfs_non_extra/1022/CAO_MBO_2018_2020_in_Word.pdf} (document ID 1022011); a separate 2020--2021 amendment agreement in the same directory carries no salary tables of its own.
  \item \textbf{p3 Extraction}: Text sections and salary-table markdown are extracted to JSON in \nolinkurl{outputs/llm_extracted/new_flow/1022/}.
  \item \textbf{Deterministic Salary Parser}: Document 1022011 parses to 273 salary rows across 30 scales and 14 scale steps, 97.4\% Tier A confidence (266 A, 7 C). Across all 14 salary-bearing documents for CAO 1022, 3,682 rows were extracted at 96.0\% Tier A+B coverage.
  \item \textbf{QA Layer 16 Verification}: Layer 16 (\nolinkurl{L16_dip_family_consistency}) corrected record 1022009's \nolinkurl{childcare_childcare_support_present} from \texttt{True} to \texttt{False}: the upstream extractor had read a general employer-policy intention clause as an explicit provision.
  \item \textbf{Derived Generosity Indices}: CAO 1022's directly stated 30.0 days of annual vacation leave standardises to $\text{leave\_numeric\_z} = -0.7389$ against the cross-CAO distribution, feeding into the record's overall composite score.
\end{enumerate}
