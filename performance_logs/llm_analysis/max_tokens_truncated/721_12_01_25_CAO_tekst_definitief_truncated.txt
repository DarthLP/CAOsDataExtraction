(Article 4.3c) An employee entering service is placed in the applicable salary scale for the function, taking into account the number of years of experience in the same or a comparable function. If the employee has no relevant work experience, placement occurs at the first step of the scale. (Article 4.4a) In case of a function change to a function valued with a higher salary scale, the salary in the new salary scale is set at least one step higher than in the old scale. (Article 4.4b) The function of qualified triage assistant, as it occurs at GP posts, will be described and valued according to the FWHZ function valuation system. (Article 4.5a) A triage assistant who obtains the VHN-recognized triage assistant diploma receives a fixed monthly allowance of 2% of the agreed base salary per month, starting from the month following the diploma acquisition. This allowance is limited to triage assistants in FWHZ scale 4 or 5. (Article 4.5b) The monthly allowance, as referred to in paragraph b, is increased to 4% two years after obtaining the diploma, if the triage assistant demonstrates consistently good performance, applied in accordance with Article 4.6. (Article 4.5c) A triage assistant who already possessed the VHN-recognized triage assistant diploma on 1 April 2010 receives the allowance referred to in paragraph b retroactively to 1 April 2010, and the allowance referred to in paragraph c two years after obtaining the diploma. The allowance is paid for the first time in August 2010, retroactively to 1 April 2010. (Article 4.5d) If the function research, as referred to in paragraph a, leads to a higher function level, the allowance referred to in paragraphs b, c, and d ceases from the date this higher function level is implemented, being the effective date of the CAO 2011. The employee concerned is then placed in the relevant salary scale. The placement is such that the salary in the new scale is at least higher than the then-current salary including the allowance. (Article 4.5e) The agreements, as mentioned in paragraphs b, c, and d, also apply to doctor's assistants working in a day practice who possess or will obtain the VHN-recognized triagist diploma. (Article 4.5f) Note: Changing employers does not affect the right to the allowance. (Page 10) The employer conducts an annual performance review with the employee. (Article 4.6a) Upon a positive review, a periodic increase is granted until the maximum of the respective scale is reached. (Article 4.6b) The periodic increase within the salary scale takes effect on the first day of the month in which the employee entered service or on the date the employee was promoted or placed in a new function. (Article 4.6c) If no performance review has taken place, the employer is obliged to grant the next periodic increase in the scale after the period referred to in paragraph c has expired. (Article 4.6d) If an employee dies, in addition to salary payment up to and including the day of death, a lump sum payment equal to 3/12th of the net annual salary is granted to: the surviving spouse (if married and not permanently separated); or, if the spouse has already died, to the minor legal, foster, or natural children jointly. (Article 5.5a) If the employee was disabled prior to death, the net death benefit is calculated based on the (full) 1/12th net annual salary paid in the month preceding the first day of disability. (Article 5.5b) If the employee was on leave prior to death, the death benefit is calculated based on the (full) net monthly salary in the month preceding the start of the leave period. (Article 5.5c) For this article, the rights of married employees and those of a partner as defined in Article 1.1 are equated. (Article 5.5d) If there are no other beneficiaries as referred to in paragraph a, the benefit is granted to the person for whom the employee was the breadwinner. (Article 5.5e) If there are no beneficiaries as referred to in the preceding paragraphs, in special cases, the benefit referred to in paragraph a may be paid to the person or persons who, in the employer's judgment, are eligible on grounds of fairness. (Article 5.5f) The death benefit, referred to in paragraph a, is reduced by the amount of the benefit due to beneficiaries in connection with the employee's death under the Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet), the Work and Income (Capacity for Work) Act (WIA) (Article 7.4), or the Disability Insurance Act (WAO) (Article 53), or a legally mandated disability insurance. (Article 5.5g) A student undertaking an internship from a recognized vocational training program receives an internship allowance of €100 gross per month. (Article 6.13a) A student undertaking an internship for less than 23 hours of the usual working time receives the allowance mentioned in paragraph a of this article proportionally. (Article 6.13b) The allowance does not apply to students who are employed and receive a salary for it. (Article 1.1) The base salary is defined as the gross monthly salary agreed between employer and employee (according to the FWHZ table). (Article 1.1) The annual salary is defined as the sum of twelve times the base salary, increased by ANW allowance, allowance for qualified triage assistants, and the average compensation for extra/additional hours over the preceding 12 months, plus 8% holiday allowance. (Article 1.1) Remuneration is according to the salary scales in Appendix 15, corresponding to the employee's function level. (Article 4.3a) The salary of an employee without a full-time employment is determined proportionally to their applicable working hours. (Article 4.3b) The function level is determined by the FWHZ function valuation system, as described in Chapter 11 of this CAO. (Article 4.3c) An employee entering service is placed in the applicable salary scale for the function, taking into account the number of years of experience in the same or a comparable function. If the employee has no relevant work experience, placement occurs at the first step of the scale. (Article 4.4a) In case of a function change to a function valued with a higher salary scale, the salary in the new salary scale is set at least one step higher than in the old scale. (Article 4.4b) The function of qualified triage assistant, as it occurs at GP posts, will be described and valued according to the FWHZ function valuation system. This function research will take place during the term of this CAO. CAO parties will make agreements based on this research on how the results will be incorporated into the CAO. (Article 4.5a) A triage assistant who obtains the VHN-recognized triage assistant diploma receives a fixed monthly allowance of 2% of the agreed base salary per month, starting from the month following the diploma acquisition. This allowance is limited to triage assistants in FWHZ scale 4 or 5. (Article 4.5b) The monthly allowance, as referred to in paragraph b, is increased to 4% two years after obtaining the diploma, if the triage assistant demonstrates consistently good performance, applied in accordance with Article 4.6. (Article 4.5c) A triage assistant who already possessed the VHN-recognized triage assistant diploma on 1 April 2010 receives the allowance referred to in paragraph b retroactively to 1 April 2010, and the allowance referred to in paragraph c two years after obtaining the diploma. The allowance is paid for the first time in August 2010, retroactively to 1 April 2010. (Article 4.5d) If the function research, as referred to in paragraph a, leads to a higher function level, the allowance referred to in paragraphs b, c, and d ceases from the date this higher function level is implemented, being the effective date of the CAO 2011. The employee concerned is then placed in the relevant salary scale. The placement is such that the salary in the new scale is at least higher than the then-current salary including the allowance. (Article 4.5e) The agreements, as mentioned in paragraphs b, c, and d, also apply to doctor's assistants working in a day practice who possess or will obtain the VHN-recognized triagist diploma. (Article 4.5f) Note: Changing employers does not affect the right to the allowance. (Page 10) The employer conducts an annual performance review with the employee. (Article 4.6a) Upon a positive review, a periodic increase is granted until the maximum of the respective scale is reached. (Article 4.6b) The periodic increase within the salary scale takes effect on the first day of the month in which the employee entered service or on the date the employee was promoted or placed in a new function. (Article 4.6c) If no performance review has taken place, the employer is obliged to grant the next periodic increase in the scale after the period referred to in paragraph c has expired. (Article 4.6d) If an employee dies, in addition to salary payment up to and including the day of death, a lump sum payment equal to 3/12th of the net annual salary is granted to: the surviving spouse (if married and not permanently separated); or, if the spouse has already died, to the minor legal, foster, or natural children jointly. (Article 5.5a) If the employee was disabled prior to death, the net death benefit is calculated based on the (full) 1/12th net annual salary paid in the month preceding the first day of disability. (Article 5.5b) If the employee was on leave prior to death, the death benefit is calculated based on the (full) net monthly salary in the month preceding the start of the leave period. (Article 5.5c) For this article, the rights of married employees and those of a partner as defined in Article 1.1 are equated. (Article 5.5d) If there are no other beneficiaries as referred to in paragraph a, the benefit is granted to the person for whom the employee was the breadwinner. (Article 5.5e) If there are no beneficiaries as referred to in the preceding paragraphs, in special cases, the benefit referred to in paragraph a may be paid to the person or persons who, in the employer's judgment, are eligible on grounds of fairness. (Article 5.5f) The death benefit, referred to in paragraph a, is reduced by the amount of the benefit due to beneficiaries in connection with the employee's death under the Sickness Benefits Act (Ziektewet), the Work and Income (Capacity for Work) Act (WIA) (Article 7.4), or the Disability Insurance Act (WAO) (Article 53), or a legally mandated disability insurance. (Article 5.5g) A student undertaking an internship from a recognized vocational training program receives an internship allowance of €100 gross per month. (Article 6.13a) A student undertaking an internship for less than 23 hours of the usual working time receives the allowance mentioned in paragraph a of this article proportionally. (Article 6.13b) The allowance does not apply to students who are employed and receive a salary for it. (Article 6.13c) An employee who has been employed by the employer for 12.5 and 25 years is entitled to a jubilee gratuity: For 12.5 years of service: a gross 1/24th annual salary. For 25 years of service: a gross 1/12th annual salary, paid net.
