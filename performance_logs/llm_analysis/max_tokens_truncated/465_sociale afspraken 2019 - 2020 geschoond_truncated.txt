Job structure update: Parties have agreed to update and actualize the current job structure during the CAO's term, including function valuation, using external expertise. This actualization will not have adverse salary consequences for employees. Progression rules: Until age 24, the employee receives an annual increment (periodiek) effective from the first salary payment after their birthday, due to an extra year of experience. From age 24, the number of years of service is the starting point, and the employee receives their salary increase every January 1st. Performance-based progression: If an employer deems an employee (older than 24) to be performing poorly, they can decide not to grant an extra increment. The employee receives a written explanation. The employee can object, and the employer must submit the objection to the Permanent Committee within fourteen days. If an employer wishes to deny a salary increase for two consecutive years, they must first request permission from the Permanent Committee. For other diplomas, the employer determines relevance; in case of disagreement, the employee can seek advice from the Permanent Committee. Maximum salary: An employee who has completed all increments and obtained all relevant diplomas reaches the maximum of their job group's salary scale. After this, they only receive general salary increases in accordance with the CAO. Holiday allowance calculation: The holiday allowance amounts to 8 percent of the actually paid wage (including any personal allowances, reimbursements, shift allowance, and fixed year-end bonus) over the year preceding May 1. The holiday allowance itself is not included in this calculation. Profit-sharing payments, bonuses, expense, and overtime reimbursements also do not count. This is without prejudice to Article 16 of the Minimum Wage Act. Payslip and annual statement: The employee receives a payslip with each payment (weekly, four-weekly, or monthly) detailing fixed salary, holiday allowance, and all allowances, reimbursements, bonuses, payments, and deductions, including government deductions. Annually, the employee receives a written statement of their wage and withheld taxes and premiums for the previous year. Job structure update: Parties have agreed to update and actualize the current job structure during the CAO's term, including function valuation, using external expertise. This actualization will not have adverse salary consequences for employees. Wage components: The wage consists of salary (at least the basic wage), any personal allowance, shift allowance, holiday allowance, and year-end bonus. Salary determination: The salary amount depends on the job group, salary scale, age (up to 24 years), or number of years of service (from 24 years). Job classifications: Each function is assigned to a job group, which corresponds to a salary scale. The job group and salary scale are stated in the employment contract. Employees can object to their assigned job group within one month; if no agreement is reached, the objection can be submitted to the Permanent Committee. Job Group 1: Functions with exclusively very simple, repetitive tasks of the same nature for which no knowledge and/or practical experience is required. These include: assistant warehouse clerk, assistant expedition clerk, administrative assistant (simple counting and arithmetic), trainee tire buffer, trainee extruder operator, trainee mechanic, assistant, trainee salesperson, trainee warehouse clerk, trainee tire buffer, trainee extruder operator, trainee mechanic, trainee inspector, junior mechanic, finisher, warehouse employee (no experience), expedition employee (no experience), assistant driver, sorter, cleaner, terrain forklift driver, warehouse employee (with some experience), expedition employee (with some experience). Salary scales: A salary scale is structured with 'increments' (periodieken). Employees progress to a higher increment within their salary scale. Salary payment frequency: Salary is always paid per week, per four-week period, or per calendar month. Performance-based progression: If an employer deems an employee (older than 24) to be performing poorly, they can decide not to grant an extra increment. The employee receives a written explanation. The employee can object, and the employer must submit the objection to the Permanent Committee within fourteen days. If an employer wishes to deny a salary increase for two consecutive years, they must first request permission from the Permanent Committee. General wage increases: Salary scales and actual paid salaries will increase by: 2.0% per November 1, 2019; 2.5% per August 1, 2020. One-time payment: The employer pays the employee a one-time gross amount of €175 in December 2019. Only employees employed on December 1, 2019, are entitled. For partial employment in 2019 or part-time employment, the payment is proportional to the duration or scope of employment. Diploma allowance: Employees in job groups 2 to 8 receive a 'diploma allowance' if they obtain a diploma relevant to their function. The allowance consists of one extra increment, effective from the first salary payment after the diploma ceremony. SVOB diplomas automatically grant this allowance. For other diplomas, the employer determines relevance; in case of disagreement, the employee can seek advice from the Permanent Committee. Maximum salary: An employee who has completed all increments and obtained all relevant diplomas reaches the maximum of their job group's salary scale. After this, they only receive general salary increases in accordance with the CAO. Holiday allowance calculation: The holiday allowance amounts to 8 percent of the actually paid wage (including any personal allowances, reimbursements, shift allowance, and fixed year-end bonus) over the year preceding May 1. The holiday allowance itself is not included in this calculation. Profit-sharing payments, bonuses, expense, and overtime reimbursements also do not count. This is without prejudice to Article 16 of the Minimum Wage Act. Payslip and annual statement: The employee receives a payslip with each payment (weekly, four-weekly, or monthly) detailing fixed salary, holiday allowance, and all allowances, reimbursements, bonuses, payments, and deductions, including government deductions. Annually, the employee receives a written statement of their wage and withheld taxes and premiums for the previous year. Job structure update: Parties have agreed to update and actualize the current job structure during the CAO's term, including function valuation, using external expertise. This actualization will not have adverse salary consequences for employees. General wage increases: Salary scales and actual paid salaries will increase by: 2.0% per November 1, 2019; 2.5% per August 1, 2020. One-time payment: The employer pays the employee a one-time gross amount of €175 in December 2019. Only employees employed on December 1, 2019, are entitled. For partial employment in 2019 or part-time employment, the payment is proportional to the duration or scope of employment. Diploma allowance: Employees in job groups 2 to 8 receive a 'diploma allowance' if they obtain a diploma relevant to their function. The allowance consists of one extra increment, effective from the first salary payment after the diploma ceremony. SVOB diplomas automatically grant this allowance. For other diplomas, the employer determines relevance; in case of disagreement, the employee can seek advice from the Permanent Committee. Maximum salary: An employee who has completed all increments and obtained all relevant diplomas reaches the maximum of their job group's salary scale. After this, they only receive general salary increases in accordance with the CAO. Holiday allowance calculation: The holiday allowance amounts to 8 percent of the actually paid wage (including any personal allowances, reimbursements, shift allowance, and fixed year-end bonus) over the year preceding May 1. The holiday allowance itself is not included in this calculation. Profit-sharing payments, bonuses, expense, and overtime reimbursements also do not count. This is without prejudice to Article 16 of the Minimum Wage Act. Payslip and annual statement: The employee receives a payslip with each payment (weekly, four-weekly, or monthly) detailing fixed salary, holiday allowance, and all allowances, reimbursements, bonuses, payments, and deductions, including government deductions. Annually, the employee receives a written statement of their wage and withheld taxes and premiums for the previous year. This actualization will not have adverse salary consequences for employees. From age 24, the number of years of service is the starting point, and the employee receives their salary increase every January 1st.
