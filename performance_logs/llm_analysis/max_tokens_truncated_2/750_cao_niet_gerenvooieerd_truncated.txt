Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). General wage increases: Wages will be increased by 1.5% on June 1, 2014, and by 0.5% on January 1, 2015. The system of price compensation remains but will not result in payment for this CAO period. Employees voluntarily affiliated with this CAO are entitled to these wage increases (Article 32). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). The salary groups I, II and III have a starting scale salary that can be applied to employees new to the sector with an employer for a maximum duration of six months. In salary groups I and II, the starting scale salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, the starting scale salary is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. (Article 27) Wage for temporary workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as temporary holiday workers (as defined in Article 1 sub 11) are determined by the 'Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968' (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). Holiday workers cannot perform duties in scale 3 or higher and are not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO (Article 28, lid 1). Wage for skilled temporary agency workers/payrollers: Temporary agency workers/payrollers who are skilled workers (as defined in Article 9 lid 2) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of their assignment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure these workers receive wages and other reimbursements according to this article and that the agency/payroll company complies with Article 100 I of the CAO, provided they do not also contribute to SFU, SFP, or similar funds (Article 28, lid 2). Transfer to a higher group or scale: For definitive placement in a higher salary scale or function group, the increase is as much as needed to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement (Article 29, sub a). Temporary function observation: An employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the temporary function and their actual wage (Article 29, sub b). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers may use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated function-year increments, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given consent according to the Works Councils Act, Article 27 lid 1-c and 1-g, and the system incorporates principles established by CAO parties. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). General wage increases: Wages will be increased by 1.5% on June 1, 2014, and by 0.5% on January 1, 2015. The system of price compensation remains but will not result in payment for this CAO period. Employees voluntarily affiliated with this CAO are entitled to these wage increases (Article 32). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). The salary groups I, II and III have a starting scale salary that can be applied to employees new to the sector with an employer for a maximum duration of six months. In salary groups I and II, the starting scale salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, the starting scale salary is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. (Article 27) Wage for temporary workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as temporary holiday workers (as defined in Article 1 sub 11) are determined by the 'Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968' (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). Holiday workers cannot perform duties in scale 3 or higher and are not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO (Article 28, lid 1). Wage for skilled temporary agency workers/payrollers: Temporary agency workers/payrollers who are skilled workers (as defined in Article 9 lid 2) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of their assignment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure these workers receive wages and other reimbursements according to this article and that the agency/payroll company complies with Article 100 I of the CAO, provided they do not also contribute to SFU, SFP, or similar funds (Article 28, lid 2). Transfer to a higher group or scale: For definitive placement in a higher salary scale or function group, the increase is as much as needed to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement (Article 29, sub a). Temporary function observation: An employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the temporary function and their actual wage (Article 29, sub b). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers may use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated function-year increments, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given consent according to the Works Councils Act, Article 27 lid 1-c and 1-g, and the system incorporates principles established by CAO parties. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). General wage increases: Wages will be increased by 1.5% on June 1, 2014, and by 0.5% on January 1, 2015. The system of price compensation remains but will not result in payment for this CAO period. Employees voluntarily affiliated with this CAO are entitled to these wage increases (Article 32). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). The salary groups I, II and III have a starting scale salary that can be applied to employees new to the sector with an employer for a maximum duration of six months. In salary groups I and II, the starting scale salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, the starting scale salary is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure these workers receive wages and other reimbursements according to this article and that the agency/payroll company complies with Article 100 I of the CAO, provided they do not also contribute to SFU, SFP, or similar funds (Article 28, lid 2). Transfer to a higher group or scale: For definitive placement in a higher salary scale or function group, the increase is as much as needed to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement (Article 29, sub a). Temporary function observation: An employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the temporary function and their actual wage (Article 29, sub b). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers may use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated function-year increments, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given consent according to the Works Councils Act, Article 27 lid 1-c and 1-g, and the system incorporates principles established by CAO parties. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). General wage increases: Wages will be increased by 1.5% on June 1, 2014, and by 0.5% on January 1, 2015. The system of price compensation remains but will not result in payment for this CAO period. Employees voluntarily affiliated with this CAO are entitled to these wage increases (Article 32). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). The salary groups I, II and III have a starting scale salary that can be applied to employees new to the sector with an employer for a maximum duration of six months. In salary groups I and II, the starting scale salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, the starting scale salary is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. (Article 27) Wage for temporary workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as temporary holiday workers (as defined in Article 1 sub 11) are determined by the 'Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968' (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). Holiday workers cannot perform duties in scale 3 or higher and are not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO (Article 28, lid 1). Wage for skilled temporary agency workers/payrollers: Temporary agency workers/payrollers who are skilled workers (as defined in Article 9 lid 2) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of their assignment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure these workers receive wages and other reimbursements according to this article and that the agency/payroll company complies with Article 100 I of the CAO, provided they do not also contribute to SFU, SFP, or similar funds (Article 28, lid 2). Transfer to a higher group or scale: For definitive placement in a higher salary scale or function group, the increase is as much as needed to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement (Article 29, sub a). Temporary function observation: An employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the temporary function and their actual wage (Article 29, sub b). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers may use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated function-year increments, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given consent according to the Works Councils Act, Article 27 lid 1-c and 1-g, and the system incorporates principles established by CAO parties. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). General wage increases: Wages will be increased by 1.5% on June 1, 2014, and by 0.5% on January 1, 2015. The system of price compensation remains but will not result in payment for this CAO period. Employees voluntarily affiliated with this CAO are entitled to these wage increases (Article 32). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). The salary groups I, II and III have a starting scale salary that can be applied to employees new to the sector with an employer for a maximum duration of six months. In salary groups I and II, the starting scale salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, the starting scale salary is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. (Article 27) Wage for temporary workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as temporary holiday workers (as defined in Article 1 sub 11) are determined by the 'Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968' (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). Holiday workers cannot perform duties in scale 3 or higher and are not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO (Article 28, lid 1). Wage for skilled temporary agency workers/payrollers: Temporary agency workers/payrollers who are skilled workers (as defined in Article 9 lid 2) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of their assignment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure these workers receive wages and other reimbursements according to this article and that the agency/payroll company complies with Article 100 I of the CAO, provided they do not also contribute to SFU, SFP, or similar funds (Article 28, lid 2). Transfer to a higher group or scale: For definitive placement in a higher salary scale or function group, the increase is as much as needed to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement (Article 29, sub a). Temporary function observation: An employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the temporary function and their actual wage (Article 29, sub b). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers may use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated function-year increments, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given consent according to the Works Councils Act, Article 27 lid 1-c and 1-g, and the system incorporates principles established by CAO parties. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager. The appraisal period covers the past calendar year. Appraisals are not transferable. The final appraisal uses a four-point scale (unsatisfactory, moderate, good, excellent) based on 3-5 objective criteria (plus individual criteria). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). Consequences: unsatisfactory = 0% increment; moderate = 50% of norm increment; good = 100% of norm increment; excellent = 150% of norm increment. For 'moderate' or 'unsatisfactory' ratings, an action plan must be created. Employees at maximum function years with an 'excellent' rating receive a bonus of 50% of the norm increment for the coming year (valid for max 1 year). If an employee cannot be appraised, they receive a one-periodic increment. If a manager fails to appraise at least 10% of their employees, they cannot receive a 'good' appraisal themselves. The Works Council's consent is required for implementation, and the 'Toetsingscommissie' (Assessment Committee) handles arbitration (Article 31). General wage increases: Wages will be increased by 1.5% on June 1, 2014, and by 0.5% on January 1, 2015. The system of price compensation remains but will not result in payment for this CAO period. Employees voluntarily affiliated with this CAO are entitled to these wage increases (Article 32). Wage settlement: Employers must provide a detailed wage statement for each employee, specifying: employee name, function title (from Jan 1, 2001), salary group/function year (if possible), payment period, gross wage and gratuity (including applicable allowances), separate CAO allowances, income tax deductions, employee's share of social insurance and Pensioenfonds Horeca & Catering premiums, employee's share of FBA and FBS contributions (if applicable), and other specified amounts (Article 33). Jubilee bonus: For 12.5, 25, or 40 years of service with an employer, an employee receives 1/4, 1, or 2 times their monthly salary, respectively. Maximum possible tax exemptions are used for payment (Article 48). The salary groups I, II and III have a starting scale salary that can be applied to employees new to the sector with an employer for a maximum duration of six months. In salary groups I and II, the starting scale salary is 10% below the applicable CAO wage, and in salary group III, the starting scale salary is 5% below the applicable CAO wage. The statutory minimum wage applies as a minimum. Function-year increments are granted on January 1 of any year if the employee was hired before October 1 of the preceding year. If a performance appraisal system is in place (with OR/personnel representation consent), wage increases may be higher or lower than one function year. More function years can be granted upon entry for prior acquired skills and experience. Employees absent due to illness for 52 weeks or longer are not entitled to function-year increments during the period of incapacity. (Article 27) Wage for temporary workers: The wage and any wage adjustments for employees working as temporary holiday workers (as defined in Article 1 sub 11) are determined by the 'Wet Minimumloon en Minimumvakantiebijslag 1968' (Minimum Wage and Minimum Holiday Allowance Act 1968). Holiday workers cannot perform duties in scale 3 or higher and are not eligible for initial price compensation under this CAO (Article 28, lid 1). Wage for skilled temporary agency workers/payrollers: Temporary agency workers/payrollers who are skilled workers (as defined in Article 9 lid 2) must be paid at the CAO level from the first day of their assignment. This includes the applicable scale salary, overtime and irregular hours allowance matrices (including holiday allowance), working time reduction, initial wage increase, expense reimbursements (clothing allowance and travel expenses, if tax-exempt), and periodic increments. The hiring employer must ensure these workers receive wages and other reimbursements according to this article and that the agency/payroll company complies with Article 100 I of the CAO, provided they do not also contribute to SFU, SFP, or similar funds (Article 28, lid 2). Transfer to a higher group or scale: For definitive placement in a higher salary scale or function group, the increase is as much as needed to bring the new scale salary in line with the equal or next higher amount in the new scale or group. This increase applies from the payment period following the placement (Article 29, sub a). Temporary function observation: An employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the scale wage at 0 function years of the temporary function and their actual wage (Article 29, sub b). Temporary function observation for young employees: A young employee temporarily performing the duties of another employee in a higher pay group (not due to vacation), if it's a full observation lasting longer than one consecutive week and not already part of their function, is entitled from the first day to an extra payment at least equal to the difference between the CAO wage of the temporary function and their own CAO wage, taking age into account (Article 29, sub c). Learning period for higher function group: An employee undergoing a learning period for placement in a higher function group (or salary scale) is not entitled to higher remuneration during this period. The learning period and its duration, maximum 3 months, must be communicated in writing (Article 29, sub d). Voluntary reduction of function level for seniors: Employees aged 55 and older can voluntarily choose a function with a lower function level. They retain pension rights based on the wage corresponding to their original function level (Article 30). Performance appraisal system and differentiated remuneration: Employers may use a performance appraisal system linked to differentiated function-year increments, provided the Works Council (or employee representation) has given consent according to the Works Councils Act, Article 27 lid 1-c and 1-g, and the system incorporates principles established by CAO parties. Key principles include: embedding in personnel policy (annual 'result and development talks' and 'progress talks'), applicability to permanent employees who have performed their function for at least 6 months, and a minimum 3-month tenure for the appraising manager.
